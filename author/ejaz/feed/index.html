What Is UAE Visa Change by Bus?

UAE visa change by bus is a road based visa status change arrangement where an eligible traveler exits the UAE through a land border, usually toward Oman, and then returns to the UAE after the new visa is approved.

The basic idea is straightforward:

Leave UAE → Complete border formalities → Enter Oman or remain in the designated border area → Process the new UAE visa → Return to UAE

For travelers in Dubai, the Hatta and Al Wajajah border route is an important land connection between the UAE and Oman. Dubai’s official information identifies Hatta as a major land crossing connecting Dubai with Oman.

A bus based visa change can be useful for tourists who want to avoid the cost of a flight while maintaining legal immigration status.

It is important to understand that this is different from simply extending a visa. A UAE visa extension may sometimes be available without leaving the country, depending on the visa category and eligibility. UAE immigration authorities provide specific extension services for eligible entry permits and tourist visas.

How Does Visa Change by Bus Work in the UAE?

The UAE visa change process normally involves several stages. The exact procedure can differ between service providers and depending on the applicant’s nationality and visa status.

1. Check Your Eligibility

Before making any travel arrangements, check whether you are eligible for a bus based visa change.

The important factors include:

  • Your nationality
  • Current UAE visa type
  • Visa expiry date
  • Passport validity
  • Eligibility to enter Oman
  • New UAE visa requirements
  • Immigration approval
  • Available bus route

Do not assume that because another traveler completed a visa change by bus, you will automatically qualify for the same arrangement.

2. Prepare Your Documents

Your passport is the most important document for the journey. Travelers should also have copies or digital versions of their UAE visa, passport and other supporting documents.

Depending on the arrangement, you may need:

  • Passport with sufficient validity
  • Current UAE visa or entry permit
  • Passport photograph
  • Oman visa or Oman entry approval, where required
  • UAE visa application documents
  • Bus booking confirmation
  • Hotel or accommodation information if required
  • Other documents requested by immigration

Oman’s official eVisa system provides a visa eligibility wizard where applicants can check requirements based on nationality and GCC residency.

3. Travel From Dubai or Another UAE Emirate

For travelers in Dubai, the journey commonly heads toward the Hatta border and the Al Wajajah side of Oman.

The Hatta crossing is approximately 140 kilometers from Dubai and is one of the main land connections between Dubai and Oman.

4. Complete UAE Exit Formalities

At the border, immigration authorities process your departure from the UAE.

A border exit fee may apply. Current travel information indicates that travelers should budget for a UAE land exit charge, with recent guides citing approximately AED 35 before applicable taxes or changes. Always confirm the current amount before departure.

5. Complete Oman Entry Requirements

After leaving the UAE, travelers must meet Oman’s entry requirements.

This is an important part of the process because Oman visa eligibility depends on nationality and circumstances. The Royal Oman Police eVisa system allows travelers to check eligibility before applying.

Travelers should never assume that crossing the UAE Oman border automatically means they can enter Oman.

6. Wait for the New UAE Visa

Once the UAE exit procedure has been completed, the new UAE visa application can be processed according to the relevant arrangement.

Processing time varies. WAYTRANSIT TRAVEL AND TOURISM LLC currently states that its bus based visa change service can involve processing from six hours onward after UAE exit, while the return bus is arranged after the new UAE visa is approved. The company also notes that immigration approval is not guaranteed.

7. Return to the UAE

After the new UAE visa is approved and the required return arrangements are confirmed, the traveler returns to the UAE.

The final entry remains subject to immigration clearance.

Dubai Visa Change by Bus: What Travelers Should Know

For people living in Dubai, Dubai visa change by bus is particularly attractive because Oman is accessible by road.

The overall journey can take several hours depending on the pickup point, traffic, border queues and visa processing.

A bus visa change is generally more suitable for people who prioritize cost over speed.

Overview

At Kumarakom, Kerala, India Valid Year-round (except blackout dates)

For GCC Wellness Travelers, Couples, Solo Retreats

Take a 14-day wellness retreat in Kerala and enjoy the calm Kerala backwaters along with the historic charm of Fort Kochi. This retreat is perfect for anyone who wants detox, relax, and refresh their mind and body with real Ayurvedic therapies, yoga, meditation, and cultural experiences.

 

Spend 11 nights at the award-winning Kumarakom Lake Resort, where skilled Ayurveda doctors guide your wellness program with personalized treatments, daily yoga and meditation, and healthy meals to make you feel strong and relaxed.

Finish your journey with 2 nights at the boutique hotel Forte Kochi, where you can relax and explore the historic streets, local markets, and traditional culture of Cochin. This Kerala Ayurveda wellness tour is a great way to enjoy wellness holidays in India, recharge your energy, and feel refreshed and healthy.

If you prefer a shorter stay, you can also check the Kumarakom Lake Resort 6 Nights and 7 Days package or the Kumarakom Lake Resort 4 Nights and 5 Days tour package

for a perfect Ayurveda holiday.

Key Highlights:
• 11 nights at Kumarakom Lake Resort in a luxury villa or suite
• 2 nights at Forte Kochi, a heritage boutique hotel in Fort Kochi
• 12 customized Ayurveda treatments over 10 days
• Initial, mid-stay & final consultations with Ayurveda doctors
• Daily yoga and meditation sessions by the lake
• All meals included at Kumarakom Lake Resort as per Ayurveda dietary plan
• Daily sunset cruises, cultural programs, and wellness activities
• Half-day guided shopping tour in Cochin (included)
• Complimentary access to all recreational amenities
• Perfectly suited for individual wellness seekers, couples, or small luxury groups

SUGGESTED ITINERARY OVERVIEW

Day 1- Arrival in Kerala & Wellness Welcome at Kumarakom Lake Resot

Arrival at Cochin International Airport (COK)
Upon arrival, you will be welcomed by our local representative and transferred in a
comfortable private vehicle to Kumarakom Lake Resort (approx. 2.5-hour scenic drive
through Kerala’s lush countryside).
Traditional Check-in at Kumarakom Lake Resort
Receive a warm traditional welcome with floral garlands, cold towels, and a refreshing
herbal drink. Proceed to check in to your preferred villa or suite, featuring heritage interiors
and modern comforts amidst a peaceful backwater setting.
Resort Orientation & Relaxation Time
Unwind and explore the 25-acre resort—stroll by the lotus ponds, enjoy the view of
Vembanad Lake, or relax in your private verandah after your journey.
Initial Ayurveda Doctor Consultation
Meet with the resort’s in-house Ayurveda physician for a detailed health consultation. Your
body constitution (Dosha) will be assessed, and a personalized treatment and diet plan will
be prescribed for the 10-day rejuvenation journey.
Evening Sunset Cruise on Vembanad Lake (Included)
Set sail on a tranquil one-hour sunset cruise through Kerala’s iconic backwaters. Let the
breeze, golden skies, and classical music set the tone for your healing journey.
Dinner at Ettukettu Traditional Restaurant
Enjoy a light, nourishing Ayurvedic dinner, customized according to your body type and
therapy plan, served in the resort’s heritage-style multi-cuisine restaurant.
Overnight Stay at Kumarakom Lake Resort
Settle into the comfort of your allotted accommodation—whether a Luxury Pavilion,
Meandering Pool Villa, or Heritage Villa with a private pool—each offering serenity and
refinement for a restful night amidst nature.

WHAT DO I NEED TO APPLY

  • A copy of your Emirates ID
  • A no objection letter from the company/sponsor of the applicant

VISA APPLICATION PROCESS
HOW TO APPLY FOR UAE VISA

STEP- 1: SELECT THE COUNTRY YOU WANT TO VISIT

  • After selecting the country which you are planning to visit
  • Choose type of Visa – For example: 30 Days UAE Visa / 30 Days Tourist Visa
  • Choose your Nationality / visitor’s nationality

Click the Search button, you will be redirected to the details page. Fill information about the visa fees. Deposit amount if applicable. Write type of visa, number of applications and the total amount you need to pay to continue with the application process.

STEP- 2: APPLICATION PROCESS

  • Verify the Type of Visa, No of Applicants, Visa Fees, Security amount if applicable and the total amount to be paid and confirm all steps.
  • Write complete Visitor details like First Name, Last Name and Nationality
  • Write your / Guarantor details – Name, Email Address and Mobile Number
  • Now click on “Continue” button to move to payment section
  • Go through the payment options. Read and accept the Terms and Conditions. Click on “Pay Now” button to do the payment.
  • Once payment is done, you will get confirmation email.
  • In order to check visa status, log into your account and find status under My Trips.

STEP- 3: SUBMIT DOCUMENTS

  • Email us your and visitor’s passport details at info@waytransittourism.com. In subject kindly mention your Visa Trip ID. Visa Trip ID can be find on the email we have sent you after you made payment successfully
  • After verification of documents and payment by our team, we will generate your visa application form. The Visa generated form would be sent to the immigration department.
  • Notice would be sent to you once your application gets approval /rejection from immigration department.
  • After the visa application approval, WAYTRANSIT TRAVEL AND TOURISM LLC will generate E-Visa. It would be sent to you via email after verification of all steps i.e. documentation and payment
  • You can Log in to the website using the User ID and Password to download the E-Visa

Kindly Note: All visas issued online will be E-Visas. If you want to get original copy, you can get by paying additional fee. A copy of E-Visa shall be provided to the customer once a round trip ticket is purchased through either our website or through Call Center / branch network else an additional service charge of AED 100 will be charged. In order to Cancel an issued visa, request can be made at an additional fee subjected to the conditions imposed by immigration department. Once your visa is approved the visa copy will be uploaded on the My Trips. You can download your E-Visa copy online by logging in to waytransittourism.com with your registered email and password. Upon receiving the E-Visa, we recommend you to check all the details mentioned in the visa copy and in case of any query please contact us immediately by emailing us at info@waytransittourism.com. It is requested to fill in all details correctly. Incorrect details may lead to visa rejection by the Immigration officer. WAYTRANSIT TRAVEL AND TOURISM LLC will not be liable for any rejection. UNDERTAKING:

You are requested to apply for a visa maximum 58 days before travel, or a minimum of seven working days before you travel. Its recommended to apply for visa in advance of travel.

The Decision to grant entry visa is entirely at the discretion of the country’s immigration authorities. WAYTRANSIT TRAVEL AND TOURISM LLC can’t guarantee your visa application will be accepted.
If I get a visa, am I guaranteed entry in to the Country?
The granting of a visa does not guarantee entry in to the country for which you have applied for the visit visa. The final decision solely depends upon decision made by the immigration officer. How long does it take to get a visa?If all required documents are submitted successfully, visa is normally issued within 5 working days. Do I have to pay any security deposit?Security deposit is applicable depending on the merit of your application, nationality and type of visa. Our visa operations team will assist you if any security deposit is required. When will my security deposit be refunded?As the visitor exits the country and the immigration authorities record the visitor’s departure, security deposit would be refunded within four international working days. In order to provide proof of departure, please check exit stamp on your passport. For rejected applications, the security deposit will be refunded within six working days of the application being rejected. Please note if you paid by credit card, you can mail us at info@waytransittourism.com or reach our team online at info@waytransittourism.com.Our representative will assist you. What documents do I need to apply for visa? Do all family members need to submit documents?Please click on Documents Required for the details. Yes, all family members need to submit documents. My passport is expiring soon – can I still get a visa?The immigration office requires all visitors to have a passport that is valid for at least six months from the date of arrival. If your passport is valid for less than six months, you will need to renew it before you can apply for a visa and travel to the desired country. Do children or infants need a visa?Yes, all passengers including children and infants on a parent’s passport, must have a valid visa to enter the country. Children below 18 are required to have visa with them. Sometimes, Immigration department waves off visa condition for parents carrying infants with them. How can I track the status of my visa application?When you complete your visa application, you will receive the TRIP ID. You need to Log in to your account and in the My Trips section you can find your visa application with the latest status. We will also notify you through email once your visa application has been approved, rejected or if additional information is required. Why was my visa application rejected?Visa Application is accepted/rejected by the decision of Immigration department. There is no influence of travelling agent on it. However, we can assist you after knowing the root cause of visa application rejection. Our team is professional and they do screening first by their own before sending to immigration department. That’s why our success rate is 99.9 %. Does the visa fee include credit card charges?No, visa fee is exclusive of credit card charges. Credit card charges will be charged separately. Will my visa fees be refunded if my application is rejected?Visa fees is not refundable; however, we will refund the deposit amount.

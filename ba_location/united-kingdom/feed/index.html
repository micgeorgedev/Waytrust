Estonia | Latvia | Lithuania | + Day Trip to Finland  Day 1-7

Embark on a 7-day journey through the charming Baltic capitals Vilnius, Riga, and Tallinn, with a Nordic twist: a day trip to Helsinki, Finland. Discover cobblestone streets, medieval castles, Baroque architecture, and modern Nordic flair all in one incredible itinerary.

What’s Included

  • 6 Nights Accommodation (4★ hotels)
  • Daily breakfast & optional dinners
  • Helsinki ferry ticket (round trip)
  • Entry fees for major landmarks
  • Local English-speaking guides
  • Airport transfers in Vilnius & Tallinn
  • Flight bookings and travel insurance

Day-by-Day Itinerary with WAYTRANSIT TRAVEL AND TOURISM LLC

Day 1 – Arrival in Vilnius, Lithuania

  • Airport pickup and hotel check-in
  • Orientation with WAYTRANSIT TRAVEL AND TOURISM LLC guide
  • Leisure stroll through Vilnius Old Town, a UNESCO World Heritage site

Day2 – Explore Vilnius

  • Visit Gediminas’ Tower, Vilnius Cathedral, and Užupis Art District
  • Optional: Trakai Castle half-day trip
  • Evening free for local shopping or cultural events

Day 3 – Transfer to Riga, Latvia

  • Scenic drive from Vilnius to Riga with photo stops
  • Explore Riga Old Town, including House of the Blackheads
  • Dinner at a traditional Latvian restaurant

Day 4 – Discover Riga

  • Visit Art Nouveau District, Riga Central Market, and Laima Clock
  • Optional: Boat cruise on the Daugava River, or Visit Jurmala Pine Forest
  • Evening at leisure or enjoy Riga’s vibrant café culture

Day 5 – Transfer to Tallinn, Estonia

  • Comfortable drive with en-route stops at picturesque towns
  • Arrive in Tallinn, check-in, and evening tour of Town Hall Square and Toompea Castle

Day 6– Helsinki Day Trip

  • Morning ferry to Helsinki, Finland (2 hours)
  • Highlights: Suomenlinna Fortress, Helsinki Cathedral, and Market Square
  • Evening ferry return to Tallinn

Day 7 – Tallinn City Tour & Departure

  • Visit Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, Kadriorg Palace, and St. Olaf’s Church
  • Transfer to Tallinn Airport for return flight

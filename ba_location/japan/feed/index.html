Tour Plan

Japan Tour Package from Abu Dhabi: Explore Tokyo, Mt. Fuji, Kyoto, and Osaka

Day 1: Tokyo

Embark on an exciting Japan tour package from Abu Dhabi as you arrive in Tokyo and check into your comfortable hotel. 

Begin your journey by visiting the iconic Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa, a must-see attraction known for its historical significance and popularity among tourists. 

Next, immerse yourself in the bustling atmosphere of Shibuya Crossing, famous for its vibrant crowds and dazzling neon lights. 

As the day winds down, experience the lively nightlife and entertainment options in the vibrant Shinjuku district.

Day 2: Mt. Fuji and Hakone

Prepare for a memorable day trip to Mt. Fuji and Hakone, two of Japan’s most celebrated natural wonders. 

Ascend to the 5th station of Mt. Fuji and be awestruck by the breathtaking panoramic views of the mountain and its surrounding landscapes.

Delight in a serene boat ride on Lake Ashi and pay a visit to the enchanting Hakone Shrine situated on the lake’s shores. 

To unwind, indulge in a relaxing soak at one of the area’s rejuvenating hot springs or onsens.

Day 3: Kyoto

Hop aboard a morning train to Kyoto, a city renowned for its majestic temples and exquisite gardens. 

Begin your day by exploring the mesmerizing Fushimi Inari Shrine, famous for its striking red torii gates stretching through the hillsides. 

Admire the dazzling Kinkaku-ji Temple, adorned with gold leaf, and then visit the UNESCO World Heritage site, Kiyomizu-dera Temple, offering a splendid view of Kyoto. 

As the day draws to a close, take a leisurely stroll through the enchanting streets of the Gion district, with its delightful geishas and old-world charm.

Day 4: Osaka

Embark on another train journey, this time to Osaka, a vibrant city renowned for its delectable cuisine and shopping delights. 

Marvel at the historical significance of Osaka Castle, representing the city’s rich history and influence. 

Then, immerse yourself in the lively ambiance of Dotonbori district, famous for its street food, bustling atmosphere, and neon lights. 

In the evening, ascend the Umeda Sky Building, a modern skyscraper offering a stunning observation deck with panoramic views of the city.

Departure:

As your unforgettable Japan tour package comes to an end, catch your flight back home, cherishing the wonderful memories of your adventures in Tokyo, Mt. Fuji, Kyoto, and Osaka.

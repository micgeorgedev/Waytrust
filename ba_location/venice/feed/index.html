Day Wise Itinerary

DAY 1: Arrival – Airport Transfer to Hotel

Your Venice adventure begins as soon as you step foot in this captivating city. Our efficient airport transfer service ensures a smooth and comfortable journey to your hotel, setting the tone for an amazing vacation.

DAY 2: Half Day Tour to the Islands of Burano Murano and Torcello (SIC) 9:00 AM

  • Will depart towards Murano. At your own leisure, you can enjoy 1.5 hours to explore the Island of Murano, famous for its glass-making and watch an exciting demonstration at a glass factory.
  • Afterwards, you will visit Burano, where you will have 2 hours of free time to meander the island’s canals and alleyways, lined with brightly colored fishermen’s houses.
  • Continue with a 1-hour visit to the sister island of Torcello. Where you can admire the Basilica di Santa Maria Assunta, with its Venetian-Byzantine mosaics or check out the peculiar Ponte del Diavolo, a bridge with no parapets!
DAY 3: Half Day Walking Tour of Venice (8:30 AM)

  • Delve into the heart of the Floating City as you are taken through the stunning historical centre of Venice. Learn about the history and culture at the same time as you experience the atmosphere of the thriving hub. You will not miss out on any of the many striking monuments and sights as you are guided through Piazza San Marco, viewing the Basilica, the Ducal Palace and the Prisons.
  • With so many amazing sights, Venice’s architectural and cultural heritage is best appreciated during a guided tour where all the research has been done for you! And the best way to savour the delightful sights is to travel by foot through the narrow streets and bustling squares of this venerable city.
  • Pause to take in the extravagant buildings which frame the Piazza San Marco. There are so many wondrous sights to take in that your eyes will not know where to look first! Competing for your attention are the towering Campanile of the Church of St Mark and the glittering Italo-Byzantine architecture of the Basilica.

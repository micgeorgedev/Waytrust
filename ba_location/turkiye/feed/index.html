Tour Plan

Turkey Tour Packages from Dubai – Explore Istanbul, Cappadocia, Pamukkale, and More!

Day 1: Istanbul

Welcome to Istanbul! Upon arrival, check into your hotel and prepare for an exciting journey through this captivating city.

Start your adventure with a visit to the iconic Hagia Sophia, a masterpiece that has transitioned from a church to a mosque and now stands as a museum, showcasing remarkable history and architecture.

Next on the list is the majestic Blue Mosque, an awe-inspiring architectural wonder that will leave you mesmerized.

In the afternoon, lose yourself in the vibrant atmosphere of the Grand Bazaar, a treasure trove of ancient charm where you can indulge in shopping for jewelry, spices, and unique souvenirs.

To conclude your day with a touch of tranquility, embark on a relaxing cruise along the Bosphorus Strait, offering picturesque views of Istanbul’s mesmerizing cityscape.

Day 2: Cappadocia

Bid farewell to Istanbul and board a morning flight to Cappadocia, an enchanting region in central Turkey renowned for its distinctive rock formations and exhilarating hot air balloon rides.

Experience the magic of Cappadocia from the skies during a hot air balloon ride, a truly unforgettable and once-in-a-lifetime experience.

Upon landing, delve into history by visiting the Göreme Open-Air Museum, a UNESCO World Heritage site boasting ancient rock-cut churches and monasteries dating back to the 10th century.

Later, immerse yourself in the artistic charm of Avanos, a town known for its exceptional pottery and ceramics.

Day 3: Pamukkale

Catch a morning flight from Cappadocia to Denizli, the gateway to Pamukkale’s natural wonders.

Pamukkale’s gleaming white travertine terraces and therapeutic hot springs await your exploration. Spend the day marveling at these unique formations, take a soothing dip in the hot springs, and wander through the fascinating ancient ruins of Hierapolis, a Greco-Roman city with an impressive historical legacy.

As the day comes to a close, settle into your hotel in Denizli for a restful evening.

Day 4: Ephesus and Izmir

Embark on a scenic morning drive to Ephesus, a captivating ancient city located near the vibrant city of Izmir.

Ephesus boasts some of the finest Roman ruins in the Mediterranean, including the renowned Library of Celsus, the Temple of Hadrian, and the majestic Great Theater.

After a captivating exploration of Ephesus, head to Izmir to savor a delectable seafood lunch and take a leisurely stroll along the charming waterfront.

Departure:

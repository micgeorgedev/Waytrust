Day-to-Day Itinerary for Your Unforgettable Hong Kong Tour

Day 1: Arrival and Hotel Transfer

Welcome to the captivating city of Hong Kong! Upon your arrival, our team will warmly welcome you and ensure your comfortable transfer to the hotel. Take a moment to relax and settle in, preparing for the exciting adventures ahead.

Day 2: Exploring the Heart of Hong Kong

Prepare for a day of exploration as we embark on a comprehensive city tour of Hong Kong’s most iconic attractions. Our journey begins with a visit to the renowned Mid-levels Escalator, followed by stops at the historic Man Mo Temple, the vibrant artistic hub of PMQ, and the cultural gem of Tai Kwun. Amidst these enriching experiences, indulge in a delightful dim sum lunch, savoring the flavors that define Hong Kong’s culinary scene.

Day 3: Lantau Island Expedition – Big Buddha and Tai O

Today, we invite you to discover the awe-inspiring beauty of Lantau Island. Our private Lantau Tour unfolds over 7 hours (or 8 hours with hotel pick-up) of captivating exploration. Begin your journey with a scenic ride on the Ngong Ping Cable Car, offering breathtaking views of Lantau Country Park and the airport. Arriving at the renowned Big Buddha, you’ll delve into the teachings of Buddhism, ascend the 268 steps to the Buddha, and explore the serene Po Lin Monastery and its Hall of 10,000 Buddhas.

From Ngong Ping, a 20-minute bus ride transports you to the enchanting Tai O, often referred to as the “Venice of Hong Kong.” Here, you’ll witness stilt houses harmoniously positioned over the water, capturing the essence of a bygone era. While once a bustling fishing community, Tai O has transformed into a charming tourist destination, renowned for its traditional shrimp paste production and vibrant seafood market.

Ngong Ping 360 Cable Car Experience

Feel the exhilaration of soaring through the sky on the Ngong Ping 360 Cable Car, offering panoramic views of Hong Kong’s natural and historical wonders. As you step onto solid ground, the culturally themed Ngong Ping Village welcomes you with a range of shops and eateries, providing a delightful shopping and dining experience.

Itinerary of kyrgyzstan tour packages

Day 1. Bishkek

 

Afternoon arrival to Bishkek. Hotel check-in after 14:00 pm. After some rest, have a city tour, visit Ala Too Square, Kurmanzhan Datka, Manas and Lenin Monuments, main governmental buildings, Old Square with Parliament House and Victory Square. Overnight in hotel.

Day 2. Bishkek – Chon-Kemin gorge. (150 km / 2-2.5 km)

After breakfast, I went to Chon-Kemin National Park. En route stop at Burana Tower. Excursion in the Historical Complex “Burana Tower”. Visit the ruins of the medieval city Balasagyn with its famous Burana Tower (IX – XII AC). Once it was one of 3 capitals of Karakhanid Empire and one of the most developed cities during the golden ages of the Great Silk Road.

After arrival, I had free time. *Optionally horse riding is available at the guest house at extra cost. Rest, dinner and overnight in guest house Kemin or similar.

(Breakfast – en-route; dinner – guest house)

Day 3. Chon-Kemin – Bokonbaevo village (180 km, 3-4 hours)

After breakfast, drive to Bokonbaevo village, where clean mountain air, lush meadows, and majestic mountain ranges will attract every lover of eco-tourism.

Afterwards in Kadzhi Sai village visit a Skazka (fairy tale) Canyon. Skazka Canyon is an area with many geological features, crazy rock formations and a variety of bizarre figures of stone and clay. It is composed of multi-colored clays, minerals and rocks that add to the scenic beauty of this place.

Some of the natural sculptures in the canyon have names. The most recognizable is the “Chinese Wall”, very reminiscent of the famous architectural monument of China. Walking around is magical. Further drive to yurt camp.

Dinner and overnight in an authentic yurt camp. 

(Breakfast – guest house, dinner – yurt camp)

Day 4. Bokonbaevo – Bishkek (300 km, 6-6.5 hours) 

Breakfast at the yurt camp. Near Bokonbaevo village meet a hunter and his bird and see scene of eagle hunting demonstration either on rabbit or on fox skin (*at extra cost). After the demonstration you will have a chance to ask the hunter about the tradition of eagle hunting and his bird features. You can take a closer look at the bird and hold it in your hands.

Upon arrival in Bishkek, hotel check-in and rest. Overnight at the hotel.

(Breakfast – hotel)

Day 5.Bishkek – Ala-Archa NP – Bishkek (90 km / 2 hours)

After a restful morning and breakfast at the resort we drove around to Ala Archa National Park.

En route it is possible to stop at cable cars (at extra cost).

After that, proceed to Ala-Archa National Park. It is a popular place for picnics, hikes, rock climbing and alpinism. There are 20 glaciers in the territory of the park. The total area of the park is 200 km2. You can go for a walk on the paved road along the river or hike up to the mountains to get a spectacular view of the gorge.

After the excursion, I returned back to Bishkek. Visit the local Osh bazaar and shopping mall. Overnight in a hotel.

(Breakfast – hotel)

Day 6. Bishkek city – airport. (35 km / 40-45 min)

  • Breakfast at a hotel.
  • Check-out till 12:00
  • Time to visit the local Osh bazaar and shopping mall. Transfer to the airport.
  • Today in Bishkek your trip concludes. (Breakfast/box – hotel)
  • Trip to the Pearl of Kyrgyzstan

This tour is for real adventure lovers. Fascinating five day tour is a unique opportunity to learn more about the most interesting places of Kyrgyzstan and meet nomads and experience night in real yurt.

During this tour you will visit the Kyrgyz pearl – Issyk – Kul lake. You will visit a lot of natural beauty. This tour will present you with a lot of memorable impressions.

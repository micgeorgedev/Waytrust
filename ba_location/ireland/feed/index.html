Ireland Tour Daily Plans 

Day 1: Arrival

Your exciting Ireland adventure begins as you touch down at the airport in Dublin, the vibrant capital city of Ireland. Upon your arrival, our friendly team will be there to greet you and provide a comfortable transfer to your hotel. Check-in, relax, and get ready for the incredible days ahead. Your journey through the Emerald Isle starts here!

Day 2: Dublin Exploration

Get ready to dive into the heart of Dublin with a panoramic Hop-on Hop-off bus tour. This tour will take you to some of the city’s most iconic sites, including:

  • Trinity College: Home to the famous Book of Kells, an illuminated manuscript dating back to the 9th century.
  • Phoenix Park: One of the largest city parks in Europe, known for its lush greenery and resident deer.
  • St. Stephen’s Green: A beautifully landscaped park in the city center, perfect for a leisurely stroll.

In the afternoon, don’t miss the chance to visit Dublin’s most popular attraction—the Guinness Storehouse. Explore the magical home of Guinness, learn about its brewing process, and enjoy a pint at the Gravity Bar while taking in panoramic views of the city.

Day 3: The Scenic Route

Prepare for a day of awe-inspiring scenery as you embark on a full-day drive through the picturesque counties of Kildare, Limerick, and Clare. Along the way, you’ll pass by:

  • Bunratty Castle: A 15th-century fortress that’s steeped in history and offers a glimpse into Ireland’s past.
  • Lahinch: A world-class golf and surf resort, where you can take in the beauty of the Atlantic coastline.

Your journey culminates at the breathtaking Cliffs of Moher, where you’ll have the opportunity to access the new Atlantic Edge visitor center and soak in the panoramic views of the rugged cliffs that plunge into the Atlantic Ocean.

In the evening, you’ll have time to explore the medieval charm of Galway City, known for its lively streets, traditional music, and warm hospitality.

Day 4: Departure

As your Ireland adventure comes to a close, you’ll check out from your hotel and bid farewell to the Emerald Isle. Our team will arrange your transfer to the airport for your departure flight. We hope you leave with cherished memories of Ireland and a deep appreciation for its natural beauty, rich history, and warm-hearted people.

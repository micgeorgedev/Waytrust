Day 1: Welcome to Yerevan!

Upon arriving at Yerevan International Airport, you will be greeted by our representative. From there, we will proceed to your hotel for check-in. Later in the evening, you will embark on a captivating panoramic city tour of Yerevan, the world’s most ancient city, boasting a history of approximately 2793 years and showcasing a unique blend of influences from Soviet Era architecture.

Designed based on Alexander Tamanyan’s plan in 1924, Yerevan exudes beauty and charm. Situated at an elevation ranging from 865 metres to 1390 metres above sea level, it has been the capital of Armenia since 1918 and proudly stands as the 13th capital of the country.

Our exploration continues to the Republic of Armenia, a lively metropolis with a rich history dating back to 782 BC. Yerevan is nestled in a valley, surrounded by picturesque hills. The southwest offers breathtaking views of the Ararat plain, dominated by the majestic Mount Ararat, adorned with eternal snow. Throughout our tour, we will visit some of the city’s main monuments, such as Yerevan Republic Square, Opera Square, and Swan Lake. We will also explore the vibrant Northern Avenue, the charming Abovyan street, known for its “Armenian Belle Epoque” architecture, and the modern Cascade Square, home to the Contemporary Art Centre and Tsitsernakaberd Memorial.

For panoramic views of the city, we will make our way to Victory Park, where the monumental statue of ‘Mother Armenia,’ a symbol of the nation, stands proudly. Additionally, we will take time to admire the mesmerising musical fountains, which enchant spectators with their water choreography perfectly synchronised with captivating music, ranging from world classical masterpieces to traditional national tunes.

After an eventful day, you will enjoy a comfortable overnight stay at your hotel in the heart of Yerevan, allowing you to rejuvenate for the adventures ahead.

Day 2 and 3: Lake Sevan – “The Pearl of Armenia” and Dilijan National Park

After breakfast, head out to the largest freshwater lake on earth in the Alpine region, Lake Sevan along with a professional English-speaking guide. Experience the beautiful expanse of the freshwater lake, the ‘Pearl of Armenia’, known for its beaches, a mediaeval monastery and exotic outdoors. The lake has two unequal parts, big Sevan and small Sevan. It is set on a narrow peninsula and also boasts of the historic monastery of Sevanavank on its northwestern part and panoramic views of the lake. It also is one of the largest high-altitude lakes in the world. Take a boat trip on the shimmering waters of Lake Sevan famous for its endemic fish type – Sevan trout, Salmo Ishkhan. Relish tasty fish of the lake with the backdrop of Lake Sevan, a highlighted thing to do.

Proceed onwards to discover Dilijan, The Armenian “Switzerland”, famous for the Armenian artisans. We will visit Haghartsin (12th -13th centuries) which is one of the wonders of Armenian Medieval Architecture. It is an important and leading scientific and cultural centre of Medieval Armenia. The beauty is in its location, nestled in the forest, surrounded by the splendour of Dilijan National Park.

Before returning to Yerevan the last stop of the day will be at Tsaghkadzor Ski Resort. Proceed up on the slope of Mount Teghenis on board the chairlift from where you get a beautiful panorama scene that opens up over the whole town of Tsaghkadzor, the valley of flowers.

Day 4: Return to Yerevan. Overnight in the pre-booked hotel in Yerevan.

While you are in Yerevan we strongly advise you to visit the Wine & Grapes Spa, especially enticing for couples. This is the first unique spa centre in Yerevan with amazing service and hospitality. The spa will rejuvenate you with the magical strength of the Ararat Valley’s sweet grapes. The wine bathing, steaming, and skin peeling with the grape seeds treatments with fresh blends are cathartic. The massage with grape oil and other procedures takes your senses to another world of ecstasy. Taste the grape honey juice and wine to toast with a divine drink.

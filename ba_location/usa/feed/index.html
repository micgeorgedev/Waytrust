Route: LAX – Anaheim – Newport Beach – San Diego – LAX
Highlights: Disneyland®, LEGOLAND®, Coastal Yachting, Luxury Shopping, SeaWorld,
Family Beach Escapes

 

Itinerary  

Day 1 : Arrival in Los Angeles – Transfer to Anaheim

Arrive at Los Angeles International Airport and be greeted by your private chauffeur who
will whisk you away to the vibrant city of Anaheim — California’s theme park capital. Settle
into your hotel and enjoy an easy evening exploring Downtown Disney, a lively promenade
filled with themed dining, shopping, and entertainment to get your vacation started on the
right note.
(B) Overnight at Westin Anaheim Resort

Day 2: Disneyland® Park Adventure
 

Step into the magic today with a full-day Park Hopper experience! Explore the original
Disneyland Park — from thrilling rides like Space Mountain to enchanting lands such as
Fantasyland. Then, cross over to Disney California Adventure Park for Pixar-themed fun,
Avengers Campus, and the incredible “World of Color” night show. Don’t forget to grab
iconic treats like churros and Mickey-shaped ice cream.
(B)Overnight at Westin Anaheim Resort

Day 3:Travel to Newport Beach – Coastal Elegance
 

After breakfast, take a short 45-minute drive to the upscale beach town of Newport Beach.
Check into your seaside resort and enjoy a leisurely afternoon exploring Balboa Island’s
boutique shops and the scenic ferry ride. For the adventurous, add a paddleboarding session
or beach cruiser bike ride along the boardwalk. End your day with a sunset dinner
overlooking the marina.
(B)Overnight at Pendry Newport Beach

Day 4: VIP Yachting Experience & Fashion Island  

Start your day with a private family yacht cruise — glide past luxury homes, playful seals,
and sparkling Pacific waters. Back on land, head to Fashion Island for a personal shopper
experience or enjoy alfresco dining in the open-air mall. Take time to relax at your hotel spa
or enjoy the family-friendly beach parks nearby.
(B) Overnight at The Resort at Pelican Hill

Day 05 :  LEGOLAND® Day & Transfer to San Diego

After breakfast, head south to Carlsbad (approx. 1.5 hours) for a day of family fun at
LEGOLAND® California. With over 60 rides, shows, and attractions — including Miniland USA and the LEGOLAND® Water Park — this theme park is perfect for kids aged 3–12. After your adventure, continue to San Diego for check-in.

(B) Overnight at Fairmont Grand Del Mar, San Diego

Day 06 :  Explore SeaWorld® or San Diego Zoo

Day 07 :  Departure from Los Angeles

After breakfast, transfer from San Diego to LAX Airport (approx. 2.5 hours) for your return
flight home. If time permits, stop at Citadel Outlets en route for some last-minute discounted
designer shopping — the perfect end to your family’s California adventure.

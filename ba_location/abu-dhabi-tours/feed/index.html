This specially curated 5 Days / 4 Nights itinerary invites guests to immerse themselves in the Ancient Kingdoms Festival (20 November – 6 December 2025). Guests will experience AlUla’s UNESCO heritage sites, cultural celebrations, and exclusive festival programs. Accommodation is at Our Habitas, offering a unique blend of luxury and cultural immersion.

 

Itinerary  

Day 1 :Arrival & Living Heritage

Morning: Arrive at AlUla International Airport. VIP meet & assist service.
Afternoon: Check-in at Our Habitas. Leisure time at the resort to unwind.
Evening: Welcome dinner at Tama Restaurant, where locally inspired dishes meet international flair.

Day 2:Nabataean Wonders
 

Morning: Vintage Land Rover tour of Hegra (UNESCO World Heritage Site) with Rawi storyteller.
Afternoon: Exclusive access to Hegra Geology Hike – exploring dramatic formations and geological wonders.
Evening: Relaxing dinner at Somewhere Restaurant in AlJadidah, offering Levantine-inspired cuisine in a vibrant setting.

Day 3:Civilisations & Cultural Trails
 

Morning: Guided visit to Dadan and Jabal Ikmah, exploring inscriptions, rock art and archaeological treasures.
Early Evening: Hegra After Dark – immersive night journey with storytelling, culinary theatre, and Nabataean heritage. Followed by the spectacular Hegra Drone show.

Late Evening: Dinner at Jontoos in Dar Tantora The House Hotel, offering heritage-infused fine dining in the heart of the Old Town.

 

Day 4:Festival Highlights & Culinary Finale  

Morning: Explore mudbrick and palmgroves in the new Old Town to Oasis: A Heritage Discovery Experience.

Afternoon: The Incense Road Experience – immersive journey of scent, trade, and culture. Followed by visit to Maraya.

Evening: Farewell dinner at Prima Classe, The Chedi Hegra, combining Mediterranean influences with AlUla’s local bounty.

 

Day 05 :  Departure 

Morning: Breakfast at Our Habitas. Optional sunrise hot air balloon ride.Transfer to AlUla International Airport for onward journey.

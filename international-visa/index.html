    Visa interview requirements vary depending on the country you are applying to, your nationality, travel history, and the purpose of your visit.     Delays can also occur due to incomplete applications, missing documents, or embassy processing backlogs. Applying early helps avoid last-minute stress and gives you enough time to handle any unexpected issues.
